Predicting bank customer churn

Can we predict which customers will leave a bank, and what drives them out? On 10,000 customers our first model looked perfect. Working out why it looked perfect turned out to be the most valuable part of the project.

When
Spring 2025
Team
4 MSBA students
Data
Kaggle Bank Customer Churn · 10,000 rows · ~20% churn
Tools
Python · pandas · scikit-learn · LazyPredict · Colab
The fake 100% Approach Results Churn drivers Recommendations My role

The fake 100%

A perfect score is a warning sign.

Our first screen of about 30 models returned 1.00 accuracy and 1.00 AUC. Instead of celebrating, we ran permutation importance and traced the result to the Complain column. It almost duplicates the outcome, possibly because complaints are logged after a customer has already decided to leave. We removed it and started again with honest numbers.

Approach

  • Cleaning: no nulls or outliers. We dropped identifier columns and Geography, and encoded Gender and Card Type.
  • Imbalance: 7,962 customers stayed and 2,038 churned. We considered SMOTE and set aside a stratified 15% holdout used only for the final test.
  • Model selection: we re-screened about 30 models with LazyPredict, then tuned the top three with GridSearchCV, optimizing for F1 to balance precision and recall on the minority class.

Results on the holdout set

Random Forest (depth 20, 50 trees)85.3%82.4%76.7%39.9%52.5%
AdaBoost (depth 5, lr 1, 50 estimators)83.1%82.5%63.7%40.2%49.3%
KNN (k = 1, Manhattan)69.9%51.5%23.1%20.3%21.6%

Random Forest had the best balance. When it flags a customer it's right about 3 times in 4, though it catches roughly 40% of churners. That's a usable shortlist for a retention team, not a complete net.

What drives churn

AdaBoost feature importance: Age (0.21), then Estimated Salary (0.12), Loyalty Points (0.12), Number of Products (0.12), Balance (0.11) and Credit Score (0.11). Older customers were more likely to leave.

Recommendations: address, prevent, sustain

  • Address: review the exit and complaint process, and confirm whether complaints are logged after customers have already decided to leave.
  • Prevent: aim retention offers at the high-importance segments, and plan outreach with marketing.
  • Sustain: retrain the model periodically. The success target is to retain 10% of customers who would otherwise churn.

My role

  • Member of a four-person team covering the full pipeline from data cleaning to business recommendations.
